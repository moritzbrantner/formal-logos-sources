### What is ALTOGETHER\_IMMATERIAL\_2, neither composed of matter and form nor a component of anything…

Source reading: [Thomas Aquinas, Summa theologiae I q.14 a.1…: What is ALTOGETHER\_IMMATERIAL\_2, neither composed of…](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/formal-logos/blob/597e4b2eb02e092f1769a2e987437bc78e14893a/corpus/claims/aquinas-st-i-q14-a1-immateriality-reason-of-knowledge.json)  
Recorded source-reading status: formalized  
Locator: I q.14 a.1 co.: immaterialitas alicuius rei est ratio quod sit cognoscitiva

Immateriality is the reason why a thing is cognitive, and the mode of knowledge follows the mode of immateriality: plants do not know because of their materiality, sense knows because it receives species without matter, intellect knows more because it is more separate from matter; God, in the highest degree of immateriality, is in the highest degree of knowledge. Read as: what is altogether immaterial, neither a matter-form composite nor a component of anything, has intellect. Not being a matter-form composite alone is not enough: a brute soul is no such composite but is a component of the animal and does not understand (I q.75 a.3, a.5; Codex P1 on #201). Interpretive step: the highest grade of immateriality is taken as intellectual.
